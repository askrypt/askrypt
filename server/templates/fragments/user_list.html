disclosures rather than a scripted dialog: the CSP forbids inline handlers, and this works with JavaScript switched off, which the pages have to. The administrator's own row deliberately offers no destructive action — `web::admin` refuses them anyway, but the safest button is the one that isn't there. The storage tier is the exception: it locks nobody out, so `admin::set_role` leaves it unguarded and every row offers it. -#}
